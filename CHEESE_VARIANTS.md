# Cheese Variant Classification Guide

> **Purpose:** Track how base cheeses become different named varieties based on aging, region, milk type, and processing methods.
> **Last Updated:** 2026-01-21

---

## How to Use This Document

When adding recipes, use this guide to:
1. Identify the correct variant name based on aging/region
2. Tag recipes with both base cheese and variant names
3. Understand the relationships between similar cheeses

---

## AGING-BASED VARIANTS

These cheeses change names or classifications based on how long they're aged.

### Gouda (Netherlands)
| Variant Name | Dutch Term | Aging Period | Characteristics |
|--------------|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Young Gouda | Jong | 4 weeks | Mild, creamy, smooth |
| Young Matured | Jong Belegen | 8-10 weeks | Slightly firmer, mild flavor |
| Matured | Belegen | 4-6 months | Firmer, more flavor |
| Extra Matured | Extra Belegen | 7-8 months | Pronounced flavor, some crystals |
| Old/Aged | Oud | 10-12 months | Sharp, crumbly, crystalline |
| Very Old | Overjarig | 12+ months | Intense, hard, caramel notes |

## REGION-BASED VARIANTS

These cheeses have the same base recipe but are named differently based on production region.

### Pecorino Family (Italy)
All made from sheep's milk (pecora = sheep), but named by region:

| Variant Name | Region | PDO | Characteristics |
|--------------|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Pecorino Romano | Lazio, Sardinia, Grosseto | Yes | Sharp, salty, hard (8+ months) |
| Pecorino Sardo | Sardinia | Yes | Milder than Romano, two types (dolce/maturo) |
| Pecorino Toscano | Tuscany | Yes | Softer, less salty, nuttier |
| Pecorino Siciliano | Sicily | Yes | Often with peppercorns, aged |
| Pecorino di Filiano | Basilicata | Yes | Cave-aged, complex |
| Pecorino Crotonese | Calabria | No | Spicy, often with chili |
| Pecorino di Picinisco | Lazio | Yes | Raw milk, grassy |

### Tomme Family (France/Alps)
"Tomme" means a round of cheese from mountain regions:

| Variant Name | Region | Characteristics |
|--------------|--------|-----------------|
| Tomme de Savoie | Savoie, France | Semi-soft, gray rind, earthy |
| Tomme des Bauges | Bauges, France | PDO, stronger than Savoie |
| Tomme de Chèvre | Various | Made with goat's milk |
| Tomme Crayeuse | Savoie | Gooey center when ripe |
| Tomme au Marc | Savoie | Washed in grape marc |
| Toma Piemontese | Piedmont, Italy | DOP, cow's milk version |

### Graviera Family (Greece)
| Variant Name | Region | Characteristics |
|--------------|--------|-----------------|
| Graviera Kritis | Crete | Sheep/goat, sweet, nutty |
| Graviera Naxou | Naxos | Cow's milk, Swiss-like |
| Graviera Agrafon | Agrafa mountains | Sheep, grassy, tangy |

### Queso de la Serena vs Torta del Casar (Spain)
Both from Extremadura, made with raw sheep's milk and cardoon thistle:

| Variant Name | Region | Characteristics |
|--------------|--------|-----------------|
| Torta del Casar | Casar de Cáceres | DOP, creamy interior, bitter notes |
| Queso de la Serena | La Serena | DOP, similar but different microflora |

## MILK TYPE VARIANTS

Same cheesemaking process, different milk sources.

### Feta Variants (by milk ratio)
| Variant Name | Milk Composition | Characteristics |
|--------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Traditional Feta | 70%+ sheep, up to 30% goat | PDO, tangy, crumbly |
| 100% Sheep Feta | All sheep's milk | Richer, creamier |
| Goat Feta-style | All goat's milk | Tangier, harder |
| "Feta-style" | Cow's milk | Not PDO, milder (often called "white cheese") |

| Mozzarella Fior di Latte | Cow's milk | Milder, firmer |

| Smoked Mozzarella | Either | Cold or hot smoked |

## PROCESSING METHOD VARIANTS

Same base cheese, different processing creates different products.

### Gorgonzola (Italy)
| Variant Name | Process | Characteristics |
|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| Gorgonzola Dolce | 50-90 days, two-curd | Creamy, mild blue, spreadable |
| Gorgonzola Piccante | 80+ days, one-curd | Firm, sharp, crumbly |

### Ricotta Variants
| Variant Name | Process | Characteristics |
|--------------|---------|-----------------|

## SIZE-BASED VARIANTS

Some cheeses are classified by wheel/form size.

### Parmigiano-Reggiano
Standard wheel: 30-40 kg (66-88 lbs)
- No official size variants, but small producers may make smaller forms

## CROSS-REFERENCE: Recipes Needed Per Variant

When adding recipes, consider that variants often need different:
- Aging conditions
- Milk processing (raw vs pasteurized)
- Cultures and enzymes
- Smoking/finishing techniques

**Priority variants for recipe development:**
1. Aging variants (timing guides)
2. Smoked variants (smoking techniques)
3. Regional variants with protected status (authentic methods)

---

## Notes

1. **PDO/AOP/DOP** = Protected designation, specific region required
2. **IGP/PGI** = Protected geographical indication, looser requirements
3. Some variants can only legally use their names if produced in specific regions
4. Home cheesemakers can make the style but may not be able to sell under protected names
5. Aging times are minimums; artisan producers often age longer
