## Essential Equipment

- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer (accurate to within 2°F)
- Cheesecloth or butter muslin
- Colander or cheese molds
- Non-iodized salt (cheese salt or kosher salt)

## Key Ingredients

### Milk
- Use whole milk for best results
- Avoid ultra-pasteurized milk (won't form proper curds)
- Raw milk produces the most flavorful cheese
- Goat and sheep milk work for most recipes

### Acids
- **Citric acid**: Quick curd formation, mild flavor
- **Lemon/lime juice**: Adds subtle citrus notes
- **Vinegar**: Neutral flavor, reliable results

### Rennet
- **Liquid rennet**: Most common, easy to measure
- **Tablet rennet**: Longer shelf life
- **Vegetable rennet**: Vegetarian alternative

## Tips for Success

1. **Temperature matters** - Invest in a good thermometer
2. **Don't rush** - Let curds set fully before cutting
3. **Save your whey** - Use for baking, smoothies, or more cheese
4. **Start simple** - Master ricotta before attempting aged cheeses
