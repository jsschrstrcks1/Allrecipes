# Homemade Brie

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 3 hours active + 4-6 weeks aging
**Yield:** One 8-inch wheel (about 2 lbs)

## Description
Brie is the "Queen of Cheeses" - a soft-ripened French cheese with a velvety white rind and creamy, buttery interior. Larger and milder than Camembert, authentic Brie develops complex mushroomy, grassy notes as it ages.

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese
- 4 gallons whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1/4 teaspoon mesophilic starter culture
- 1/8 teaspoon Penicillium candidum
- 1/16 teaspoon Geotrichum candidum
- 1/2 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/4 cup water
- 1/2 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 1 tablespoon cheese salt (non-iodized)

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot (at least 5 gallons)
- Thermometer
- Long knife
- Slotted spoon and ladle
- 8-inch Brie mold (or Saint-Paulin mold)
- Cheese mats
- Ripening box with humidity control

## Instructions

### Day 1: Make the Curds

#### Step 1: Culture the Milk
1. Heat milk to 90°F (32°C)
2. Add calcium chloride, stir well
3. Sprinkle cultures on surface, wait 2 minutes
4. Stir gently to incorporate
5. Cover and ripen at 90°F for 60 minutes

#### Step 2: Add Rennet
1. Add rennet solution, stir gently for 1 minute
2. Cover and maintain 90°F for 90 minutes
3. Check for clean break - curd should be firm

#### Step 3: Cut and Heal
1. Cut curds into 3/4-inch cubes
2. Let rest undisturbed for 5 minutes
3. Very gently stir once or twice over 10 minutes
4. Let curds settle to bottom

#### Step 4: Mold
1. Place mold on mat over draining tray
2. Gently ladle curds into mold
3. Fill completely - curds will settle significantly
4. Add more curds every 30 minutes as they compact
5. Let drain at room temperature overnight

### Day 2: Flip and Begin Drying

#### Step 5: Flip
1. Flip cheese in mold every 2-3 hours
2. Cheese will reduce to about 2 inches tall
3. Continue flipping until cheese holds shape

### Day 3: Salt

#### Step 6: Salt and Start Aging
1. Remove from mold
2. Salt top surface, let sit 10 minutes
3. Flip and salt other side and edges
4. Place in ripening box at 55°F, 95% humidity
5. Keep lid cracked for air exchange

### Week 1-2: Develop Bloom

#### Step 7: Monitor Rind Development
1. Flip daily
2. White mold should appear by day 5-7
3. By day 14, should have full white coverage
4. Wipe away any unwanted colored mold with brine

### Week 3-6: Ripen

#### Step 8: Final Ripening
1. Once fully covered, wrap in cheese paper
2. Continue at 50-55°F
3. Flip weekly
4. Test by pressing edge - should give slightly
5. Young (4 weeks): firmer, milder
6. Ripe (6 weeks): soft, runny, stronger flavor

## Storage
- Wrap in cheese paper or wax paper
- Refrigerate up to 2 weeks once ripe
- Freezing not recommended

## Tips
- Brie ripens from outside in - wait until center softens
- The rind is completely edible
- If rind becomes slimy, humidity is too high
- Strong ammonia smell means over-ripe

## Classic Preparations
- Baked Brie en croûte (in puff pastry)
- With honey and toasted nuts
- On a cheese board with grapes and crackers
- Melted over steak or burgers
