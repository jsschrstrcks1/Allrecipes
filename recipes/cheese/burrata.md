# Homemade Burrata

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 2 hours
**Yield:** 4 burrata balls (about 4 oz each)

## Description
Burrata is a fresh Italian cheese from Puglia - a pouch of mozzarella filled with stracciatella (shredded mozzarella mixed with cream). When cut open, the creamy center oozes out. It's mozzarella's more luxurious cousin.

## Ingredients

### For the Mozzarella Shell
- 1 gallon whole milk
- 1.5 teaspoons citric acid dissolved in 1 cup cool water
- 1/4 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 1 teaspoon cheese salt

### For the Stracciatella Filling
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella (reserved from shell-making)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- Pinch of salt

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer
- Slotted spoon
- Heat-proof gloves
- Large bowl of ice water
- Large bowl of hot salted water (for stretching)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Curds
1. Pour milk into pot
2. Add citric acid solution while stirring
3. Heat slowly to 90°F (32°C)
4. Add rennet solution, stir for 30 seconds
5. Continue heating to 105°F (41°C)
6. Curds will separate from whey

### Step 2: Form the Curd Mass
1. When curds form a solid mass, remove from heat
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Use slotted spoon to transfer curds to colander
4. Press gently to release whey

### Step 3: Stretch the Mozzarella
1. Heat salted water to 170-180°F (77-82°C)
2. Cut curds into 1-inch pieces
3. Place curds in hot water
4. Let sit 1 minute
5. Using gloved hands, stretch and fold like taffy
6. Work until smooth and shiny

### Step 4: Make the Stracciatella
1. Reserve about 1/3 of the stretched mozzarella
2. Shred it into small pieces
3. Mix with cold heavy cream
4. Add pinch of salt
5. Stir gently - should be stringy and creamy
6. Refrigerate while forming shells

### Step 5: Form the Shells
1. Divide remaining mozzarella into 4 portions
2. Reheat each portion in hot water as needed
3. Stretch into a thin, flat disc (about 6 inches)
4. Work quickly while still pliable

### Step 6: Fill and Seal
1. Place disc in your palm
2. Add 2-3 tablespoons stracciatella to center
3. Gather edges up around filling
4. Twist and pinch to seal completely
5. Place seam-side down
6. Dip in ice water briefly to set shape

### Step 7: Store
1. Place in container with a little cream or whey
2. Use within 24-48 hours for best quality
3. Bring to room temperature before serving

## Tips
- Speed is essential - mozzarella becomes unworkable when cool
- Keep reheating curd pieces in hot water as needed
- The shell should be thin but without holes
- Seal well or the filling leaks

## Serving
- Serve at room temperature
- Drizzle with olive oil and flaky salt
- Top with fresh tomatoes and basil
- With prosciutto and arugula
- On grilled bread
- Simply with crusty bread to soak up the cream
