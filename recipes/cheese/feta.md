# Homemade Feta Cheese

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 2 hours active + 5 days aging
**Yield:** About 1 pound

## Description
Feta is a brined Greek cheese with a tangy, salty flavor and crumbly texture. Traditional feta is made from sheep's milk, but this cow's milk version is delicious for salads, pastries, and Mediterranean dishes.

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese
- 1 gallon whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures (or 1/8 tsp mesophilic culture)
- 1/4 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 1/4 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/4 cup water (if using store-bought milk)

### For the Brine
- 1/2 gallon water
- 1 pound cheese salt (non-iodized)
- 1 tablespoon white vinegar

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer
- Long knife
- Slotted spoon
- Cheese mold or colander
- Cheesecloth
- Container for brining

## Instructions

### Day 1: Make the Curds

#### Step 1: Culture the Milk
1. Heat milk to 86°F (30°C)
2. Add calcium chloride solution and stir
3. Add yogurt or culture and stir thoroughly
4. Cover and ripen at 86°F for 1 hour

#### Step 2: Add Rennet
1. Stir in rennet solution using up-and-down motions
2. Cover and let set for 1 hour at 86°F
3. Test for clean break - curd should split cleanly when cut

#### Step 3: Cut and Rest
1. Cut curds into 1/2-inch cubes
2. Let rest for 10 minutes
3. Gently stir curds for 20 minutes
4. Let curds settle to bottom for 5 minutes

#### Step 4: Drain and Mold
1. Line a colander or mold with cheesecloth
2. Ladle curds into the mold
3. Let drain for 2 hours at room temperature
4. Flip the cheese, re-wrap in cheesecloth
5. Continue draining for 4-6 more hours

### Day 2: Salt and Dry

#### Step 5: Dry Salting
1. Remove from mold
2. Cut into 2-inch blocks
3. Sprinkle all sides generously with cheese salt
4. Place on a rack over a tray
5. Let sit at room temperature for 3 days, flipping twice daily
6. Salt lightly each time you flip

### Day 5+: Brine and Age

#### Step 6: Make the Brine
1. Dissolve salt in water (should taste very salty)
2. Add vinegar
3. Chill to refrigerator temperature

#### Step 7: Brine the Feta
1. Place cheese blocks in a container
2. Cover completely with brine
3. Refrigerate for at least 5 days before eating
4. Flavor improves with age - up to several months

## Storage
- Keep submerged in brine in the refrigerator
- Properly brined feta lasts 2-3 months
- If too salty, soak pieces in milk for 30 minutes before using

## Tips
- The longer it brines, the tangier and saltier it becomes
- For authentic flavor, use a mix of sheep and goat milk
- Temperature control is key - too warm and it becomes bitter
- If mold forms on the brine surface, skim it off - cheese underneath is fine

## Uses
- Greek salad
- Spanakopita (spinach pie)
- Watermelon feta salad
- Crumbled on pizza
- Baked feta pasta
- Stuffed peppers
