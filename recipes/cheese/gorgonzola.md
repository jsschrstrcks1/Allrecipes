# Homemade Gorgonzola-Style Blue Cheese

**Difficulty:** Advanced
**Time:** 4 hours active + 2-3 months aging
**Yield:** About 4 lbs

## Description
Gorgonzola is Italy's famous blue cheese from Lombardy and Piedmont. It comes in two styles: Dolce (sweet and creamy) and Piccante (firm and sharp). This recipe creates a Dolce-style cheese with creamy texture and mild blue flavor.

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese
- 4 gallons whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1/4 teaspoon mesophilic starter culture
- 1/4 teaspoon thermophilic starter culture
- 1/8 teaspoon Penicillium roqueforti
- 1/2 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/4 cup water
- 1/2 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 3 tablespoons cheese salt (non-iodized)

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot (6+ gallon)
- Thermometer
- Long knife and curd harp
- Slotted spoon
- Gorgonzola molds (tall, narrow) or Camembert molds
- Cheese needles or skewers
- Aging cave or dedicated refrigerator

## Instructions

### Day 1: Morning Milk

#### Step 1: Ripen Evening Milk
1. Heat 2 gallons milk to 86°F (30°C)
2. Add half the mesophilic culture
3. Cover and let ripen overnight at room temperature

### Day 2: Make the Cheese

#### Step 2: Combine and Culture
1. Next morning, heat remaining 2 gallons to 86°F
2. Add ripened milk from Day 1
3. Add remaining cultures and Penicillium roqueforti
4. Stir and let ripen 30 minutes

#### Step 3: Add Rennet
1. Add calcium chloride, stir
2. Add rennet, stir gently for 1 minute
3. Cover and let set 60 minutes
4. Test for clean break

#### Step 4: Cut and Work Curds
1. Cut into 1-inch cubes
2. Let rest 10 minutes
3. Very gently stir for 20 minutes
4. Let curds settle

#### Step 5: Drain and Layer
1. Drain off most whey
2. Line molds with cheesecloth
3. Add curds in layers, salting between each
4. Don't press - keep curds loose
5. Let drain at 70-75°F for 24 hours
6. Flip every 3-4 hours

### Day 3: Continue Draining

#### Step 6: Form the Cheese
1. Continue flipping every 4 hours
2. Cheese should compact on its own
3. After 24 hours total, remove from mold
4. Rub surfaces with remaining salt

### Day 4-7: Prepare for Aging

#### Step 7: Develop Rind
1. Place on mat in aging space
2. Keep at 55°F, 95% humidity
3. Turn twice daily
4. Salt lightly each day

### Week 2: Pierce

#### Step 8: Create Blue Veining
1. Pierce cheese from top to bottom (30+ holes)
2. Pierce horizontally from all sides
3. Use thin skewers or special needles
4. These holes allow oxygen for blue mold

### Weeks 3-12: Age

#### Step 9: Develop Flavor
1. Continue at 40-45°F, 95% humidity
2. Turn twice weekly
3. Blue veins should appear by week 3-4
4. For Dolce style: 2 months minimum
5. For Piccante style: 3+ months

## Storage
- Wrap in foil
- Refrigerate up to 3 weeks
- Let soften 30 minutes before serving

## Tips
- Traditional Gorgonzola uses two batches of milk (two curds)
- High humidity is essential for blue development
- The two-day milk process creates complex flavor
- Dolce should be spreadable; Piccante crumbles

## Serving Suggestions
- With pears and walnuts
- In risotto
- On polenta
- With honey on gnocchi
- In creamy pasta sauces
- On pizza with figs
