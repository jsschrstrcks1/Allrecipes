# Homemade Gouda

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 4 hours active + 2-18 months aging
**Yield:** About 2 lbs

## Description
Gouda is a Dutch semi-hard cheese named after the city in the Netherlands. Known for its smooth, creamy texture and sweet, nutty flavor, Gouda ranges from young and mild to aged with caramel notes and crunchy protein crystals.

## Ingredients
- 2 gallons whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1/4 teaspoon mesophilic starter culture
- 1/4 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/4 cup water
- 1/2 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- Cheese salt for brine

### For the Brine
- 1 lb cheese salt
- 1/2 gallon water
- 1 tablespoon calcium chloride
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer
- Long knife
- Slotted spoon
- Gouda or tomme mold
- Cheese press
- Cheese wax (for coating)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Culture the Milk
1. Heat milk to 90°F (32°C)
2. Add calcium chloride, stir well
3. Sprinkle mesophilic culture on surface
4. Let rehydrate 2 minutes, stir in
5. Cover and ripen 10 minutes

### Step 2: Add Rennet
1. Add rennet solution
2. Stir gently top-to-bottom for 1 minute
3. Cover and let set 45 minutes
4. Test for clean break

### Step 3: Cut and Stir
1. Cut curds into 1/2-inch cubes
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Stir gently for 10 minutes
4. Let curds settle to bottom

### Step 4: Wash the Curds (Key Step!)
1. Remove 1/3 of the whey (about 2-3 quarts)
2. Replace with the same amount of 175°F water
3. This raises temperature and washes out lactose
4. Final temperature should reach 100°F (38°C)
5. Stir for 20 minutes at this temperature

### Step 5: Mold and Press
1. Drain whey
2. Transfer curds to cloth-lined mold
3. Press at 10 lbs for 20 minutes
4. Flip, press at 20 lbs for 20 minutes
5. Flip, press at 40 lbs for 12 hours

### Step 6: Brine
1. Make saturated brine solution
2. Add calcium chloride and vinegar
3. Chill to 50-55°F
4. Brine for 3 hours per pound of cheese
5. Flip halfway through

### Step 7: Air Dry
1. Remove from brine, pat dry
2. Air-dry at room temperature for 2-3 days
3. Turn twice daily
4. Rind should feel dry to touch

### Step 8: Wax or Natural Rind
**For Waxed Gouda (recommended for beginners):**
1. Heat cheese wax to 225°F
2. Dip or brush onto completely dry cheese
3. Apply 2-3 coats

**For Natural Rind:**
1. Skip waxing
2. Brush with brine weekly during aging
3. Rind will develop naturally

### Step 9: Age
- **Young (2-3 months)**: Mild, creamy, slightly sweet
- **Medium (4-6 months)**: More pronounced flavor, firmer
- **Aged (6-12 months)**: Nutty, complex, developing crystals
- **Extra-Aged (12-18 months)**: Intense, caramel notes, crunchy crystals

Age at 55°F, 80-85% humidity. Turn weekly.

## Variations

### Smoked Gouda
Cold smoke for 4-6 hours before waxing

### Cumin Gouda
Add 2 tbsp cumin seeds to curds before molding

### Herbs de Provence Gouda
Add 2 tbsp dried herbs to curds

## Storage
- Waxed whole wheel: Cool, dark place for months
- Cut: Rewax cut face or wrap tightly; refrigerate

## Tips
- The "washing" step is what makes Gouda mild and sweet
- More washing = sweeter cheese
- Waxing prevents moisture loss during aging
- Aged Gouda develops crunchy tyrosine crystals

## Serving Ideas
- Young: Sandwiches, snacking
- Aged: Cheese boards, shaved on salads
- Smoked: With beer, on burgers
- Any age: Melts beautifully in dishes
