# Homemade Halloumi

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 2 hours
**Yield:** About 1 pound

## Description
Halloumi is a semi-hard Cypriot cheese famous for its high melting point. You can grill, fry, or bake it without losing its shape, making it perfect for vegetarian main dishes.

## Ingredients
- 1 gallon whole milk (or mix of cow and goat milk)
- 1/4 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 2 tablespoons cheese salt (non-iodized)
- Fresh mint leaves (optional, traditional)

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer
- Long knife
- Slotted spoon
- Cheesecloth
- Cheese mold or makeshift mold
- Heavy weight for pressing

## Instructions

### Step 1: Heat and Set
1. Heat milk to 95°F (35°C) in a large pot
2. Remove from heat
3. Stir in rennet solution for 30 seconds
4. Cover and let set for 45 minutes to 1 hour
5. Check for a clean break

### Step 2: Cut and Cook the Curds
1. Cut curds into 1/2-inch cubes
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Slowly heat to 104°F (40°C) over 30 minutes
4. Stir gently to prevent curds from matting
5. Remove from heat, let curds settle 5 minutes

### Step 3: Drain and Press
1. Line a mold with cheesecloth
2. Transfer curds with a slotted spoon
3. Press with 10 lbs weight for 1 hour
4. Flip cheese, re-wrap, and press 1 more hour
5. SAVE THE WHEY - you'll need it!

### Step 4: Cook the Cheese (The Magic Step)
1. Heat the whey to 185-195°F (85-90°C)
2. Cut pressed cheese into 3-inch blocks
3. Add cheese blocks to hot whey
4. Cook for 30-45 minutes until blocks float
5. Remove with slotted spoon

### Step 5: Season and Shape
1. While still warm, flatten blocks slightly
2. Sprinkle with salt and dried mint (if using)
3. Fold each piece in half, pressing to seal
4. Fold is traditional but optional

### Step 6: Brine and Store
1. Make brine: Dissolve 1/2 cup salt in 4 cups whey
2. Cool brine to room temperature
3. Place halloumi in brine
4. Refrigerate - it's ready to eat now but improves over 1-2 days
5. Keeps 2 weeks in brine

## Cooking Methods

### Pan-Fried Halloumi
1. Slice 1/4-inch thick
2. Heat dry non-stick pan over medium-high
3. Cook 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown
4. Serve immediately - it hardens as it cools

### Grilled Halloumi
1. Cut into thick slices
2. Brush lightly with olive oil
3. Grill over medium-high heat 2-3 minutes per side
4. Drizzle with lemon juice and herbs

### Baked Halloumi
1. Place slices in baking dish
2. Top with cherry tomatoes, olives, herbs
3. Bake at 400°F for 15-20 minutes

## Tips
- The whey-cooking step is what makes halloumi squeaky and un-meltable
- Mix cow and goat milk for more authentic flavor
- Fresh halloumi is milder; it gets tangier with age
- If too salty, soak in plain water for 30 minutes

## Serving Ideas
- Halloumi and watermelon salad
- Grilled halloumi burger
- Halloumi fries with yogurt dip
- Mediterranean mezze platter
- Breakfast with eggs and tomatoes
