# Homemade Mascarpone

**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Time:** 20 minutes active + 12 hours draining
**Yield:** About 1 lb

## Description
Mascarpone is a luxuriously rich Italian cream cheese from Lombardy. With its silky texture and delicate sweetness, it's essential for tiramisu and many Italian desserts. This is one of the simplest cheeses to make.

## Ingredients
- 4 cups heavy cream (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice OR
- 1/4 teaspoon tartaric acid dissolved in 1 tbsp water

## Equipment
- Double boiler OR pot with glass bowl
- Thermometer
- Whisk
- Fine-mesh strainer
- Cheesecloth or butter muslin
- Bowl for draining

## Instructions

### Step 1: Set Up Double Boiler
1. Fill bottom pot with 2 inches of water
2. Bring to a gentle simmer
3. Place bowl or top pot over water
4. Add heavy cream

### Step 2: Heat Cream
1. Heat cream slowly to 185°F (85°C)
2. Stir occasionally to heat evenly
3. Do not let it boil
4. Use thermometer - temperature is crucial

### Step 3: Add Acid
1. Add lemon juice or tartaric acid solution
2. Stir gently for 30 seconds
3. Continue heating at 185°F for 5 minutes
4. Cream will thicken slightly

### Step 4: Cool
1. Remove from heat
2. Let cool at room temperature for 30 minutes
3. Cover and refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight

### Step 5: Drain
1. Line strainer with damp cheesecloth (4 layers)
2. Place over bowl
3. Pour thickened cream into strainer
4. Cover loosely
5. Refrigerate and drain 8-12 hours

### Step 6: Finish
1. Transfer mascarpone to container
2. Whisk briefly until smooth
3. It should be thick but spreadable
4. Use immediately or refrigerate

## Storage
- Store in airtight container
- Refrigerate up to 1 week
- Do not freeze - texture suffers

## Tips
- Tartaric acid gives a cleaner flavor than lemon
- The higher the cream fat content, the better
- Don't rush the draining - patience yields better texture
- If too thick, whisk in a tablespoon of cream

## Classic Uses
- Tiramisu (the essential ingredient)
- Mixed with espresso and cocoa
- Frosting for cakes
- Stuffed in cannoli
- Spread on fruit tarts
- Whipped with honey for berries

## Savory Applications
- Stirred into risotto
- Pasta sauce base
- Spread on crostini
- Mixed into mashed potatoes
