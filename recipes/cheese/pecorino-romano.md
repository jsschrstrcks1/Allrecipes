# Homemade Pecorino Romano

**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Time:** 4 hours active + 8-12 months aging
**Yield:** About 3 lbs

## Description
Pecorino Romano is one of Italy's oldest cheeses, dating back over 2,000 years to ancient Rome. Made from sheep's milk, it has a sharp, salty, tangy flavor that's essential for pasta dishes like cacio e pepe and amatriciana.

## Ingredients
- 3 gallons whole sheep's milk (or substitute with goat milk)
- 1/4 teaspoon thermophilic starter culture
- 1/2 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/4 cup water
- 3/4 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- Cheese salt for rubbing and brining

### For the Brine
- 2 lbs cheese salt
- 1 gallon water
- 1 tablespoon calcium chloride

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot
- Thermometer
- Long knife and curd harp
- Slotted spoon
- Hard cheese mold (tomme-style)
- Cheese press
- Aging space (cave or dedicated refrigerator)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Culture the Milk
1. Heat sheep's milk to 95°F (35°C)
2. Add calcium chloride, stir well
3. Sprinkle thermophilic culture on surface
4. Let rehydrate 2 minutes, then stir
5. Cover and ripen 30 minutes at 95°F

### Step 2: Add Rennet
1. Add rennet solution
2. Stir gently for 1 minute
3. Cover and let set 30 minutes
4. Test for clean break

### Step 3: Cut and Cook Curds
1. Cut curds into 1/4-inch cubes
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Slowly raise temperature to 116°F (47°C) over 45 minutes
4. Stir constantly to prevent matting
5. Curds should become small and firm

### Step 4: Drain and Mold
1. Let curds settle 5 minutes
2. Drain off whey
3. Transfer curds to cloth-lined mold
4. Press at 10 lbs for 30 minutes
5. Flip, press at 20 lbs for 2 hours
6. Flip, press at 30 lbs for 12 hours

### Step 5: Make Brine
1. Dissolve salt in water
2. Add calcium chloride
3. Chill to 55°F
4. Brine should be saturated (salt won't dissolve)

### Step 6: Brine the Cheese
1. Place cheese in brine
2. Brine for 12 hours per pound of cheese
3. Flip halfway through
4. A 3 lb wheel needs about 36 hours

### Step 7: Dry and Age
1. Remove from brine
2. Pat dry
3. Let air-dry at room temperature for 2-3 days
4. Turn daily until rind feels dry

### Step 8: Long Aging
1. Age at 55°F, 85% humidity
2. Turn twice weekly
3. Rub with olive oil monthly (optional, traditional)
4. Minimum 8 months for table cheese
5. 12+ months for grating quality
6. Rind will darken to tan/brown

## Storage
- Keep whole wheel in cool place
- Once cut, wrap tightly and refrigerate
- Grated Pecorino freezes well

## Tips
- Sheep's milk gives authentic flavor; cow's milk lacks tang
- High cooking temperature makes the cheese firm and gratable
- Long aging develops the sharp, salty character
- The longer it ages, the harder and more pungent

## Traditional Uses
- Cacio e Pepe (pasta with cheese and pepper)
- Pasta alla Gricia
- Amatriciana sauce
- Carbonara (traditional Roman version)
- Grated over beans and soups
- With pears and honey as dessert
