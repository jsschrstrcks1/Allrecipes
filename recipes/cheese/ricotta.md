# Homemade Ricotta

**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Time:** 30 minutes
**Yield:** About 2 cups

## Description
Ricotta means "recooked" in Italian - traditionally made from leftover whey. This version uses whole milk for a richer, creamier result. Perfect for lasagna, cannoli, or spreading on toast.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 gallon whole milk
- 1 cup heavy cream (optional, for extra richness)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice or white vinegar

## Equipment
- Large pot
- Thermometer
- Fine-mesh strainer or colander
- Cheesecloth
- Large bowl

## Instructions

### Step 1: Heat the Milk
1. Line a strainer with cheesecloth and set over a large bowl
2. Combine milk, cream (if using), and salt in a large pot
3. Heat over medium heat to 190°F (88°C), stirring occasionally
4. Do not let it boil

### Step 2: Add Acid
1. Remove from heat
2. Stir in lemon juice or vinegar gently
3. Let sit undisturbed for 5 minutes
4. You should see curds forming and separating from the whey

### Step 3: Drain
1. Gently ladle the curds into the lined strainer
2. Let drain for 10-60 minutes depending on desired consistency:
   - 10 minutes: Creamy, spreadable ricotta
   - 30 minutes: Traditional ricotta texture
   - 60 minutes: Firm, dry ricotta for baking

### Step 4: Store
1. Transfer to an airtight container
2. Refrigerate for up to 1 week
3. Stir before using if liquid separates

## Variations

### Herbed Ricotta
Add 2 tablespoons fresh herbs (basil, chives, thyme) after draining

### Honey Ricotta
Stir in 2 tablespoons honey for a sweet spread

### Whey Ricotta (Traditional)
Use leftover whey from mozzarella making:
1. Heat 1 gallon whey to 200°F
2. Add 1/4 cup vinegar
3. Skim off the delicate curds that form
4. Drain as above (yield will be smaller)

## Tips
- Fresh lemon juice gives the best flavor
- Don't squeeze the curds - let gravity do the work
- Save the whey for bread baking or smoothies

## Uses
- Lasagna filling
- Stuffed shells
- Cannoli cream (mix with powdered sugar and chocolate chips)
- Toast topping with honey and fruit
- Pancake batter enrichment
- Gnocchi
