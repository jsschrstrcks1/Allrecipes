# Homemade Stilton-Style Blue Cheese

**Difficulty:** Advanced
**Time:** 5 hours active + 9-12 weeks aging
**Yield:** About 8 lbs

## Description
Stilton is England's "King of Cheeses" - a rich, creamy blue cheese with a natural crusty rind. Known for its complex flavor of butter, nuts, and tangy blue veining, authentic Stilton can only be made in three English counties. This recipe creates a similar style cheese.

## Ingredients
- 6 gallons whole milk (not ultra-pasteurized)
- 1/4 teaspoon mesophilic starter culture
- 1/8 teaspoon Penicillium roqueforti
- 1/2 teaspoon calcium chloride in 1/2 cup water
- 3/4 teaspoon liquid rennet dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
- 4 tablespoons cheese salt (non-iodized)

## Equipment
- Large stainless steel pot (8+ gallons)
- Thermometer
- Long knife
- Large slotted spoon
- Stilton molds (tall, cylindrical) or large tomme molds
- Cheese mat and draining tray
- Piercing needles
- Aging cave at proper humidity

## Instructions

### Day 1: Make the Curds

#### Step 1: Culture the Milk
1. Heat milk to 86°F (30°C)
2. Add calcium chloride, stir well
3. Sprinkle mesophilic culture on surface
4. Add Penicillium roqueforti
5. Let rehydrate 2 minutes, stir in
6. Cover and ripen 90 minutes at 86°F

#### Step 2: Add Rennet
1. Add rennet solution
2. Stir gently for 1 minute
3. Cover and let set 90 minutes
4. Test for clean break - should be very firm

#### Step 3: Cut and Rest
1. Cut curds into 1/2-inch cubes
2. Let rest undisturbed 15 minutes
3. Gently stir for 10 minutes
4. Let curds settle 30 minutes
5. Drain off whey to curd level

#### Step 4: Mill the Curds
1. Let curds mat together for 30 minutes
2. Cut matted curds into 1-inch strips
3. Stack and turn every 15 minutes for 2 hours
4. Break into walnut-sized pieces
5. Mix in salt

### Day 1-5: Mold and Drain

#### Step 5: Fill Molds
1. Place molds on draining mat
2. Add curds loosely - don't compress
3. Curds should remain open for blue veining
4. Let drain at 68-72°F

#### Step 6: Turn Daily
1. Turn cheese in mold twice daily
2. Do not press - gravity only
3. Continue for 5 days
4. Cheese will compact naturally

### Day 6-7: Smooth the Rind

#### Step 7: Remove and Smooth
1. Carefully remove from mold
2. Smooth sides by hand
3. Rub surfaces with remaining salt
4. Place on mat in ripening area

### Weeks 2-5: Develop Rind

#### Step 8: Initial Aging
1. Age at 55°F, 95% humidity
2. Turn daily
3. Natural rind will form
4. Don't pierce yet

### Week 6: Pierce

#### Step 9: Create Blue Veining
1. Using long needles, pierce from top to bottom
2. Make 40-50 holes spaced evenly
3. Pierce from sides as well
4. This introduces oxygen for blue mold

### Weeks 7-12: Final Aging

#### Step 10: Ripen
1. Continue at 50-55°F, 90% humidity
2. Turn twice weekly
3. Blue veining develops over weeks 7-10
4. Cheese softens from outside in
5. Ready at 9-12 weeks

## Storage
- Wrap in wax paper, then foil
- Refrigerate - keeps 4 weeks once cut
- Serve at room temperature

## Tips
- Traditional Stilton is NEVER pressed
- The loose curd structure allows blue veins
- High humidity is critical
- Rind should be dry but not cracked
- Blue mold needs oxygen - don't wrap too early

## Traditional Serving
- With port wine
- With walnuts and celery
- Crumbled into salads
- Melted into sauces
- With crusty bread
- At the end of a proper British meal
